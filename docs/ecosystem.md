# pear, peel, and The Commons

**Status:** ecosystem architecture direction. Only the standalone,
non-AI pear concept walkthrough is implemented in this repository.

## Three lanes, distinct duties

| Lane | Purpose | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| **pear** | Standalone, non-AI worker-support foundation, with optional access to The Commons | Interactive concept prototype |
| **peel** | Local, lightweight AI companion that adapts alongside each worker's journey, practice, and needs | Proposed; not implemented |
| **The Commons** | Collective layer for human discussion, peer support, and shared practice knowledge | Proposed; not implemented |

## pear — standalone, non-AI foundation

pear provides local-first preparation, private reflection, explicit
saving, refusal, export, and deletion. Its core must remain useful
without models, network access, or Commons participation.

pear may offer deliberate, worker-initiated access to The Commons.
That connection does not turn private reflections into shared material
or make participation compulsory.

## peel — local, lightweight AI companion

peel is the AI harness to be developed with workers. It would adapt
practitioner-reviewed support alongside each worker's explicitly
chosen journey, practice, language, access needs, and boundaries.

“Evolves with the worker” must not mean silently profiling or training
on them. Adaptations should be worker-inspectable, correctable,
exportable, resettable, and refusable.

Private reflections must not automatically become prompts, training
data, shared resources, or organizational intelligence. Local,
lightweight processing is a design requirement to evaluate—not a
claim that local operation automatically guarantees privacy or safety.

peel must remain optional. Declining it must not diminish access to
pear, human support, or The Commons.

## The Commons — collective human layer

The Commons is the human discussion and peer-support space. It may
also hold deliberately authored and reviewed practice resources,
corrections, limitations, provenance, and disagreement.

It must not become an AI substitute for peer support, an engagement-
metrics platform, or a private-to-public publishing pipeline.

Contribution, moderation, cultural review, and peer support are human
labour and relationships. Participation must not silently authorize
model training or secondary use of contributed material.

## Connections and boundaries

- No automatic flow from private reflection to peel or The Commons.
- No automated crisis response, worker scoring, employer surveillance,
  endurance optimization, or simulated human peer.
- No model training on private worker or service-user material.
- pear remains complete without peel or The Commons.
- peel does not replace the human relationships held in The Commons.
- Any future real-case or direct-care proposal requires its own
  authority, governance, consent, evaluation, and stop conditions.

## Fellowship-scale implementation principle

The ecosystem is the long-term vision. A first Fellowship milestone
should prove one coherent worker journey and one bounded peel
capability, compared against pear's no-model pathway.
