# Service-user impact and informed-choice canvas

**Purpose:** challenge a proposed capability before implementation. This canvas is not consent, legal advice, an ethics approval, or evidence that deployment is justified.

**Capability:**  
**Proposed product:** `pear / (s)pear / separately governed commons`  
**Assessment owner and accountable decision-makers:**  
**Status:** `not proposed / rejected / investigation / limited test / suspended`

## 1. Necessity and alternatives

- What specific problem and affected practice are we addressing?
- Who defined the problem, and who benefits?
- What happens without software? What non-model/non-data alternative exists?
- Why is this capability necessary rather than merely convenient?
- What burdens, throughput expectations, or new work could it create?

- Worker/operator population:
- Service users and other affected non-users:
- Who may be unable to refuse or challenge safely?
- Institutional, legal, professional, cultural, disability, language, racial, class, gender, age, and economic power:
- How will workers and people with lived experience of receiving support govern and evaluate this, with appropriate compensation?

## 4. Accessible information

For each affected person, specify how they learn:

- what is used and its material role in support;
- exact information received and generated;
- human and organisational recipients;
- processing location, providers, and jurisdictions;
- retention, exports, backups, and deletion limits;
- refusal and a genuinely human, tool-free alternative;
- correction, contestation, complaint, and accountable humans;
- legal/institutional access that cannot honestly be ruled out.

## 5. Relational and epistemic effects

- Could attention move from the person to the interface?
- Could a form or output freeze an early interpretation?
- Are source language, observation, interpretation, unknowns, and disagreement preserved?
- Could culture, language, disability, anger, spirituality, or survival responses be pathologised?
- Could generated text simulate empathy, confirm error, or conceal uncertainty?
- Who remains responsible when worker, organisation, developer, host, and provider disagree?
